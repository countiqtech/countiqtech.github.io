Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

This notice explains how CountIQ Technologies LLP handles information in connection with its public website, countiq.in. It does not cover clinical use of SurgiCount or processing of patient records.

1. Information and purposes

When you visit the website, hosting and external resource providers may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser details and requests to deliver and secure the site. If you contact us by email, we may process your name, email address, message and other information you choose to provide to respond to your inquiry and manage business correspondence. Please do not send patient records or confidential clinical information through website contact links.

2. Optional website analytics

With your permission, we use Google Analytics 4 to understand visits, traffic sources and engagement. Analytics may process device and browser information, approximate location, page views, interactions and cookie identifiers. We use this information to understand and improve the website. The website code disables Google signals and advertising personalization signals for this analytics tag.

Analytics is optional. The home page asks for your choice before loading the analytics tag. It stores that choice in your browser's local storage. If you accept, Google Analytics may set cookies such as _ga and _ga_*.

3. Your analytics choices

You can decline analytics or change your choice at any time using Analytics preferences in the footer of the home page. Declining disables the site's analytics tag and attempts to remove its analytics cookies. You can also clear cookies and local storage in your browser. Withdrawal does not undo processing that has already occurred.

4. Third-party services

The site uses GitHub Pages for hosting, Google Fonts for typography, and unpkg to deliver the model-viewer software. These services may receive technical request information independently of your analytics choice. Google Analytics is loaded only after acceptance. External links, including Gmail contact links, lead to services with their own privacy practices.

See Google's Privacy Policy, Google's explanation of partner-site data use and GitHub's Privacy Statement.

5. Sharing, retention and international processing

Information may be processed by service providers supporting the website, analytics and business communications, or disclosed when required by law. Providers may process information outside India. Analytics retention depends on the configured Google Analytics settings and Google's service practices. Business correspondence may be retained as needed to respond to inquiries, maintain business records and meet legal obligations.

6. Privacy requests

You may contact us to request access, correction or deletion of personal information, withdraw consent where applicable, or raise a privacy concern. Requests will be considered in accordance with applicable law and may require verification of identity.

7. Contact and updates

For privacy questions or grievances, contact CountIQ Technologies LLP at founder@countiq.in. We may update this notice as our website or data practices change. The latest version will be available on this page.
