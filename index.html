Why we started

Surgical counting hasn't changed enough. We're working to change that.

Surgical teams must account for instruments, needles, sponges, gauze, and other materials introduced during a procedure. In complex operating rooms, accurate counting still depends heavily on repeated manual verification and team coordination.

Meet SurgiCount

A physical platform built around surgical accountability.

SurgiCount is being designed as a mobile operating-room platform that combines computer vision, intelligent sensing, and real-time workflow visibility.

01

AI-Assisted Visibility

Computer vision supports identification and monitoring within designated accountability zones.

02

Intelligent Sensing

Integrated sensing provides an additional layer of physical item-awareness during the workflow.

03

Real-Time Accountability

Counts, item status, and discrepancy awareness are brought into one continuous view.

04

Operating-Room Focused

The platform is being shaped around mobility, workflow practicality, and surgical-team visibility.

The idea in one line

See more. Verify more. Miss less.

SurgiCount is being developed to add a technology-supported layer of visibility to existing surgical counting practices — without replacing clinical judgement or established safety protocols.
